About the property

Description property

Tower Green has been thoughtfully designed as a welcoming neighbourhood of spacious 2, 3 and 4-bedroom homes which offer generous light-filled accommodation, designed to maximise light and space to the highest specification with comfortable living spaces, contemporary kitchen, generous bedrooms and luxurious bathrooms, surrounded by landscaped open spaces and wide pathways that foster community spirit. Each home combines intelligent layouts with modern finishes, blending energy efficiency, comfort and enduring style to create a premium living environment.

At Tower Green, interiors are shaped for modern living, with spaces that work beautifully day to day. Open-plan kitchens form the hub of each home, with streamlined cabinetry, quality appliances, and generous workspace for cooking, dining and gathering. Bathrooms are finished with modern tiling and fittings for a fresh, timeless look. Bedrooms are spacious and light-filled, with well-planned layouts and practical storage.

Every detail is considered for comfort, durability, and style, creating homes that feel effortless to live in and easy to enjoy.

These stunning 3-bedroom homes offer a spacious 121 sq. m / 1,302 sq. ft of living space, these A2-rated homes combine modern design with superior energy efficiency, ideal for today’s family lifestyle. The open-plan kitchen/living area is perfect for family meals and social gatherings, with a separate utility room for added storage and convenience.

For more information or to register your interest visit www.towergreen.com
